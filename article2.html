The past is a golem

Blood looks like the past. Everything I knew would haunt me does. Every meaningless childhood thing every tiny big meaningless suffering did not leave me after all. I dream of it like it was yesterday, Years have gone by, but the past persist. Tortured times in the past I relive with a new play, is that healing? But I don’t need to “heal”, don’t want to, every desperate bygone day with different choices and different people—I visit nothing as my past, I am only as I am now, only my surroundings have regressed, but I stay current, with all my powers—I am far too old to dream of school days so frequent and fervently. Anyone’s suffering from when they were 15 should be long gone from the mind by 21—and they are, never do I consciously dwell on anything—but my unconscious must run a different track. Time is after all different in dreams. It is closer together. The past is breathing down your neck while the present dissolves into the future, shriveling up in your hands like horror—I am no longer in horror of myself, I wake from a writhing dream and don’t know yet who I’m waking up as. I can only move forward and I do—in my waking life. But in my dreams, I am running backwards, and I cannot control it. There is power in the past I know, but it is slipping by me, I am not wise enough to harness it, that my life may not be linear—that is my worst fear, and it always has been.

As one prays with kavanah, one must live life with a certain kavanah also—only now am I doing it, and like a new shoe—no, there is only blood. The world can tell I am waking up, they notice it—not just the deepening voice, the narrowing of the face, something of the countenance of me is different, and people are looking, Nothing is stopping me now—is this really how all other people live their lives from the beginning? The absence of suffering does not bring ecstasy like I thought it would, it is just that—the absence of suffering. And by its very nature, I cannot feel bad from it.

The past is a golem. It lumbers blind and has no sense. There is nothing I can do to invigorate it with will, it is both living and dead, but it lives in an eternal circle. Is living in a circle death? It certainly is to some.

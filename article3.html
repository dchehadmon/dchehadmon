A mirror we watch the world go by in

History explains everything. Religion is just a prism of confirmation bias through which to perceive history. I don’t know anything except that I am transgender. It is the only thing I can be sure of in the entire world.

It’s not too late to rethink my entire damn life. I’m caught on a hamster wheel chasing something I’m not sure is real or true at all, and never actually was. Even the whole Christianity shit, like, okay they have their interpretations post-factum projecting their shit—but so do we? What does it matter even? It’s all lies, spooned down to us, we can never know just how. Stupid literate monkeys. How could I trust my perception of God, any divine intervention in my life whatsoever, at all, considering what was integrated into me by all outside forces from the start? I used God as an alternative to my parents, a world to have of my own, something totally without them, free form them, why I felt the need to have this, I don’t know. By the time I was 6 or 7 though, this way of thinking was clear to me. It only hardened with time. A crazy cope like everything is. And now what? I can’t resolve this in an hour, a day. Do I really know if one way of living is superior to the other? We are judging based on what life-way gives us the most real happiness, satisfaction, because that’s what we believe God wants form us. Honestly, source? It's what we want from us, and it’s how we evaluate the validity/superiority of our life-way as opposed to others. Having sex or jacking off whenever with whoever, do we actually know this makes a clear-minded responsible person mentally worse off than doing the frum tahares mishpacha thing?

So much of halachic life-way justification hinges off some kind of assumption that it’s actually better for us than an alternative, but what if we look at the people keeping it all and don’t see utopia or even that they are doing much better than anyone else, we just see stifling and cover-up stuff and contradictions. We don’t actually know that it’s better, we just have to think it is because it’s what fits with the rest of our worldview, if it isn’t actually better, then did the Torah lie? Did God not actually want the best for us? What was the purpose of the torah at all then? it’s just vibes and confirmation bias with everything we have to lose if we’re wrong. I’ve genuinely never actually had a religious experience. I just have shit I’m interested in and also want acceptance from others. God happens to be one for the things I’ve always been interested in. Monkey shit honestly. Everything is a creation of our minds. A mirror we watch the world go by in. Even the transgender thing, maybe someday they’ll figure out what caused it, what kind of mind-body mishap that wasn’t metaphysical at all. I don’t know.

I sleep, drink, I feel pain, I feel pleasure. I don’t feel God as a separate thing like these, a real thing. For all I can figure it’s some ghost conjured by all these things trying to co-exist with each other. It’s not that I don’t think that God exists, or that there’s not a creator or anything even. It’s that we humans have any accurate perception of it or that it ever interacted with us the way Judaism or any other religion said it did or does.
